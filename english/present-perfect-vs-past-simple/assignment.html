Present Perfect Vs Past Simple — assignment

Present Perfect vs. Past Simple — English Grammar Assignment In this assignment, students practise the difference between the Present Perfect and Past Simple tenses. They will review sentence structure, helping verbs, past participles, life experiences, and time expressions through 20 multiple-choice questions. Students will learn how to use have/has + past participle in the Present Perfect and how to choose between the two tenses in real-life situations. The assignment includes questions about specific past times, such as yesterday, last year, and on Friday, as well as Present Perfect expressions such as ever, never, before, and just. Level: A2–B1 English learners Duration: 15–20 minutes Total questions: 20 Total score: 20 points
