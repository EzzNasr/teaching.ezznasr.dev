Ace the Interview — assignment

In this assignment, students prepare for a successful job interview by reviewing key interview vocabulary and practising clear, professional answers. They will identify effective interview responses, match common questions with suitable answers, improve weak answers, and write their own responses to typical interview questions. The assignment focuses on introducing yourself, describing strengths and experience, explaining career goals, discussing teamwork, answering challenging questions, and asking appropriate questions at the end of an interview. Students are encouraged to use complete sentences, specific examples, and positive professional language.
